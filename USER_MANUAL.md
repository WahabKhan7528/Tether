# Tether: The Official User Manual

Welcome to **Tether**, your private digital sanctuary. We understand that love sometimes spans across different cities, time zones, or simply the busy hours of the day. Tether was designed with a single philosophy in mind: to keep you **closer, despite the distances**. 

Unlike sprawling social media platforms, Tether is a quiet, intimate space designed exclusively for two. There are no followers, no public feeds, and no distractions—just a beautifully crafted environment to preserve every memory, thought, and note for just the two of you.

This manual will guide you through the rich features of your new shared space, ensuring that you can make the most of every moment.

## 1. Getting Started: The Pairing

Tether is strictly a two-player experience. To begin building your world, you must pair your accounts. The pairing process is secure and happens in an instant.

1. **Create an Account:** Start by signing up using your email and a secure password. You will be prompted to set up your basic profile details, like your name and a profile picture.
2. **Generate Your Invite Code:** If you are the first to sign up, you will be presented with a unique 6-digit Ethereal Code. This code is the key to your shared space.
3. **The Connection:** Share this 6-digit code with your partner via your preferred messaging app. When your partner creates their account, they simply select "I have an invite code" and enter the 6 digits.
4. **Instantly Linked:** The moment the code is entered, your two profiles will be atomically linked. From that second forward, every photo uploaded, every letter written, and every mood updated is shared exclusively between you two.

## 2. The Dashboard: Your Shared Hub

Whenever you open Tether, you are greeted by the Dashboard. This is the living, breathing center of your relationship. It is designed to give you a comforting overview of your shared life at a single glance.

- **The Greeting:** A warm, personalized greeting that changes based on the time of day.
- **Recent Activities:** A quick scroll through the most recent memories you've uploaded or letters you've exchanged, keeping the best moments at the forefront of your mind.
- **Shared Bucket List:** A snapshot of your mutual goals. Whether it is "Travel to Japan" or "Learn to cook pasta from scratch," you can check off milestones together directly from the Dashboard.
- **Quick Actions:** Easy access buttons to quickly log a new memory, write a letter, or add an idea to the jar.

## 3. Partner Status: Silent Signals

Sometimes, you don't need a text message to know how your partner is feeling. The **Partner Status Widget** on your Dashboard allows you to softly communicate your mood, bridging the gap when words fall short.

- **Setting Your Mood:** On your Dashboard, click on "My Mood." A beautiful, animated overlay will appear asking, "How are you feeling?" You can select from options like Happy, Sad, or other custom emotions.
- **Real-Time Sync:** Because Tether is built on real-time technology, the moment you select a mood, it updates on your partner's Dashboard instantly—even if they are halfway across the world. 
- **The Intention:** It is a quiet, non-intrusive way to say, "I'm thinking of you," or "I had a hard day and could use a hug."

## 4. Memories & The Journey Map

Photographs capture a moment in time, but in Tether, they map out the journey of your relationship.

### Logging a Memory
1. Navigate to the **Memories** tab and click **New Memory**.
2. **Upload:** You can upload multiple high-resolution photos seamlessly. 
3. **Details:** Add a romantic title, write a detailed description of how the day made you feel, and select the exact date it happened.
4. **Location Tagging:** This is the most crucial step. Type in the location of the memory. By adding a location, it becomes part of your Journey Map.

### Our Places (The Map)
Navigate to the **Map Tab** to view a beautiful, interactive globe. Every memory or gallery photo you have ever tagged with a location will appear here as a custom Ethereal marker.
- **Exploration:** Zoom out to see the global spread of your adventures, or zoom in to a specific city where you've pinned a dozen coffee dates.
- **Interactive Pins:** Clicking on any pin on the map opens a preview of the memory, complete with the first photo, the title, and a direct link to read the full story. Watch as the pins slowly populate across the world, mapping the physical footprint of your love story.

## 5. Daily Reflections (Prompts)

We believe that asking the right questions brings people together, sparking conversations that might not happen on an ordinary Tuesday.

- **The Daily Prompt:** Every day, a new, thought-provoking question appears in the **Reflections** tab. It might be lighthearted ("What is a silly habit I have that makes you smile?") or deep ("When did you first realize you were in love?").
- **The Lock Mechanism:** To encourage honest, unbiased answers, your partner's response remains locked and obscured until you submit your own. 
- **The Reveal:** Once both of you have bravely submitted your answers, the lock breaks, and both responses are revealed side-by-side. It is a daily ritual designed to foster intimacy and understanding.

## 6. The Ideas Jar

Never again struggle to answer the question, "What should we do for our next date?" The Ideas Jar is a whimsical, interactive feature designed to bring spontaneity back into your relationship.

- **Adding Ideas:** Have a sudden romantic idea? Toss it into the **Ideas Jar**. You can add a catchy title and a brief description of the plan.
- **The Physics of the Jar:** The jar is not just a list; it is a beautifully animated, physics-based glass orb filled with colorful slips of paper representing your ideas.
- **Drawing from the Jar:** When you cannot decide what to do, visit the jar. Click **Shuffle** and watch the ideas physically bounce around the glass. Then, click **Draw from the Jar!** The app will randomly select one of your ideas for your next adventure, presenting it with a burst of sparkles.

## 7. Scrapbook Letters

For thoughts that require more than a quick text message, use the **Letters** feature. This is your space to write long-form, thoughtful notes that your partner can cherish forever.

- **Writing:** Open the Letter Editor to compose your thoughts. It features a rich-text editor allowing you to format your words perfectly.
- **Templates:** You can choose from carefully crafted visual themes to set the perfect tone for your words:
  - *Classic:* Clean, traditional formatting.
  - *Elegant:* Serif fonts and sophisticated margins.
  - *Minimal:* Stripped back, focusing entirely on the words.
  - *Vintage:* Sepia tones and typewriter aesthetics.
  - *Scrapbook:* Playful, allowing you to attach images directly from your shared memories.
- **Reading:** When your partner receives the letter, it opens as a beautifully formatted, full-page experience that feels as personal and deliberate as receiving physical mail.

## 8. The Gallery & Reels

A continuous, seamless feed of your visual and digital life together.

### The Gallery
- **Masonry Grid:** A stunning, Pinterest-style masonry grid displaying every single photo ever uploaded to Tether. This includes photos attached to specific Memories, as well as photos uploaded directly to the gallery.
- **Lightbox Viewer:** Click on any image to open the full-screen lightbox for an immersive, distraction-free viewing experience. You can swipe or use arrow keys to navigate through your entire visual history.

### Saved Reels
- Found a short video on Instagram or TikTok that reminded you of them? Paste the link into the **Reels** tab to save it. 
- Tether automatically organizes these into a grid of clickable thumbnails. Now you have a shared, categorized collection of videos to laugh at or dream about together, without losing them in a sea of direct messages.

## 9. Shared Radyo

Music is the heartbeat of a relationship. The **Radyo** feature allows you to soundtrack your shared space, listening to the same tunes.

- **Uploading Tracks:** You can upload your favorite audio files directly to the platform. This could be "our song," a playlist you curated, or even an intimate voice note you recorded just for them.
- **The Global Player:** Once you press play on a track, a floating mini-player appears at the bottom of your screen. This player is *persistent*—meaning the music continues uninterrupted as you navigate through different pages of the application. It is a shared ambient soundtrack that plays softly in the background as you read their letters or explore your map.

## 10. Profile & Interactions

Your individual identities are beautifully merged into one couple profile, while still allowing for personal expression.

### Personal Customization
- Set your personal nickname, gender, and a short bio.
- **Favorite Color:** Pick a color that represents you.
- **Avatar:** Upload a beautiful profile picture that will appear next to all your memories and letters.

### The Couple Identity
- Set your shared relationship status (e.g., "Dating," "Engaged," "Married").
- Highlight your **Anniversary Date**, ensuring it is always remembered.
- Write a bio for yourselves as a couple.

### Digital Affection
- Feeling affectionate but don't have time to write a full letter? Send a digital **Hug** or **Kiss** directly from the profile page. 
- The profile maintains silent counters that track every hug and kiss sent, serving as a subtle, numerical reminder of your ongoing affection.
